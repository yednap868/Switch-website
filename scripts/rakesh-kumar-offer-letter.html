Engagement: Your title shall be Operations Manager (Full-Time) and your date of joining and reporting date shall be on or before 14-August-2026 in Gurugram. During the tenure of your employment with the Company, you shall be required to work exclusively for the Company unless you obtain prior written consent from the Company. You agree to provide services exclusively to the Company and not to enter into any form of employment or contract with other organization.

Salary: So long as you remain actively employed by the Company, you will be eligible for the salary as prescribed in the attached Annexure A. Your salary less applicable deductions will be paid in accordance with the applicable laws of India. Your salary may change at the discretion of the Company, upon advance written notice to you. TDS, PF and ESIC or any other statutory liabilities (if any) falling within your salary structure shall be subject to mandatory employment deductions as per the statutory norms.

Appraisals: In the course of the employment and on an annual basis, depending upon your performance, the Company's performance and your contribution to the Company's growth, and other factors which may be taken into account by the Company, the Company may revise the Salary payable to you.

Bonus: You will also be eligible to be paid a discretionary bonus amount at the end of the relevant financial year. Such bonus amount(s) are determined at the Company's sole discretion. To be eligible you must be employed with the Company and must not have given or received notice of termination at the time the bonus awards are made. Entitlement to a bonus is not automatic and you acknowledge that you may receive a nil bonus.

Probation: You would be on probation for a period of 90 days following the commencement of employment. Your confirmation at the end of the probation period is subject to the discretion of the Company.

Notice Period: The notice period applicable during probation for all employees across all positions joining the Company is 1 month. The notice period after confirmation shall be 1 month or as the Company's compliance team will decide.

Resignation: If you resign from your position with the Company, you are required to provide written notice of resignation and serve a notice period as stipulated above.

Termination: The Company may elect (but shall not be obliged) to terminate your employment prior to the expiry of the notice period and make a payment in lieu of notice, unless your employment is terminated for “Cause”, in which case the Company shall have the right to terminate your employment with immediate effect and with no requirement for notice period or payment in lieu thereof.

“Cause”, as used herein, means:

  1. your material neglect or material failure to perform your job duties and responsibilities,
  2. your failure or refusal after due notice, to comply with lawful policies or directives of the Company or its designated official(s),
  3. your material breach of any contract or agreement between you and the Company and/or your material breach of any statutory duty, fiduciary duty or any other obligation that you owe to the Company,
  4. your commission of an act of fraud, theft, embezzlement or other criminal offence against the Company, or your engaging in unprofessional, unethical or other intentional acts that materially discredit the Company or are materially detrimental to the reputation, character or standing of the Company, or
  5. your conviction with respect to any crime punishable under the laws of India.

Please note that employment is for no fixed term and either you or the Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time for any reason, in accordance with this Agreement.
