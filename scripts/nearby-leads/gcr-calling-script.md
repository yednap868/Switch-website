# Switch — Golf Course Road Calling Script

Use this while working `switch-golf-course-road-MOBILE-callsheet.xlsx`.
Every number is an **owner/manager mobile**. WhatsApp first, then call. Log the
result (Interested? / Hours / Order Confirmed?) after **every** contact.

---

## The offer — say it exactly like this
- **Verified, local staff in 24 hours** — from their own area.
- **Book by the shift: 1–12 hours, billed per hour.** (No monthly, no weekly.)
- **Pay only after the worker reports** — no advance.
- **Instant replacement** if the worker doesn't show or isn't a fit.

Three risk-reducers to lean on: *pay-after · replacement · staff in 24 hrs.*

---

## Golden rules (before you dial)
1. **WhatsApp first**, then call — higher pick-up, they've seen your name.
2. **30 seconds to a question.** Don't pitch for a minute — get them talking fast.
3. **Route to the decision-maker:** *"Sir, aap owner/manager se baat karwa denge? Staff ke baare mein baat karni thi."*
4. **One goal per call:** learn their staffing need + get a "haan" to send someone.
5. **Log it.** A call you didn't record is a lead you'll lose.

---

## Universal call flow

**1. Open (10 sec)**
> "Namaste sir, main [YOUR NAME] Switch se. 30 second loonga —
> hum aapke area ke **verified staff** dete hain, **24 ghante ke andar.**"

**2. Discover (ask, then shut up and listen)**
> "Abhi aapke yahan **kis kaam ke bande ki kami** hai?"

**3. Lock the details** (the moment they name a need)
- Kitne log? *(how many)*
- Konsa role? *(which role)*
- **Kitne ghante? (1–12)** ← this is the billing unit
- Kab / kis din chahiye?
- Shift timing? *(e.g. 10am–7pm)*

**4. Close**
> "Theek hai sir, main **aaj hi [2] log [8] ghante ke shift** ke liye bhej deta hoon.
> Aap bas dekh lijiyega. **Paisa tabhi jab banda aa jaye.** Deal? 🤝"

Get the yes → mark **Order Confirmed? = Yes** + Hours.

## Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| **"Abhi zaroorat nahi"** | "Koi baat nahi sir — kabhi turant chahiye ho toh ek message. Number save kar leta hoon. Aam taur pe kitne logon ki team chalti hai?" *(plant seed + learn churn)* |
| **"Charge kya hai?"** | "Bilkul transparent sir — advance kuch nahi, pay tabhi jab banda aa jaye. Per-hour rate WhatsApp pe bhej dun details ke saath?" |
| **"Staff tikta nahi / bhaag jaata hai"** | "Sir aap sirf shift ke liye le rahe ho, 1–12 ghante — lambe commitment ka tension nahi. Aur banda na aaye toh **instant replacement.**" |
| **"Mahine ke liye chahiye"** | "Sir hum monthly nahi, **shift ke hisaab se** dete hain — jis din jitne ghante chahiye bula lo. Zyada flexible aur sasta. Roz chahiye toh roz bhej denge." |
| **"Pehle se vendor hai"** | "Badhiya sir — humein **backup** rakhiye. Jab unka banda nahi aata, hum 24 ghante mein cover karte hain. Ek baar try karke dekhiye." |
| **"Bharosa kaise karein?"** | "Har banda **Aadhaar-verified** hai, aur paisa aap **kaam ke baad** dete ho — risk aapka zero hai." |
| **"WhatsApp pe bhejo"** | "Abhi bhejta hoon 👍" → send the WhatsApp opener, call back in 10 min. |

---

## No answer / call back later
- **No answer:** send the WhatsApp opener, mark *No answer*, retry once later in the day.
- **"Call back"** (busy): "Sir kab call karun — 1 ghante baad?" Mark *Call back* + note the time.
- **Wrong number:** mark *Wrong number* so it's skipped.

>
> • Sirf shift ke liye: **1–12 hours, per-hour billing**

> Aaj bhej dun? Bas timing bata dijiye. ⚡
