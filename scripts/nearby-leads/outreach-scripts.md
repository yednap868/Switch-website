# Switch B2B Outreach Scripts — 2-Day Order Blitz

**Goal:** 10 confirmed staffing orders in 2 days.
**Channel order:** WhatsApp first (highest reply rate) → Call the openers → Walk-in for the closest ones.
**What we offer:** staff by the **shift — 1 to 12 hours, billed per hour. No monthly, no weekly.**

**No free shifts.** Risk-reducers we DO offer instead:
- 💰 **Pay only after the staff joins** (no advance)
- 🔁 **Instant replacement** if the worker doesn't show or isn't a fit
- ⚡ **Staff in 24 hours**, from your own area
- ⏱️ **Book just the hours you need** — pay only for those hours

Use the `2-day-blitz-callsheet.csv` and update the **Status / Next Action** columns after every contact.

---

## 1. First WhatsApp message (send to all P1 leads)

**Tone: professional, direct, respectful. Not over-familiar.**

> Namaste 🙏 Main [Name], Switch se.
> Switch businesses ko **verified staff** deta hai — helper, waiter, kitchen staff, housekeeping, guard — Gurugram mein, **24 ghante ke andar**.
>
> • Sirf shift ke liye: **1–12 hours, per-hour billing**
> • Payment kaam ke baad — koi advance nahi
> • Worker na aaye ya fit na ho to **instant replacement**
>
> Aapke business ke liye staff chahiye ho to bataiye — kitne log aur kitne ghante? Main aaj hi arrange kar deta hoon.

**Shorter alt (for busy owners):**

> Namaste, [Name] from Switch.
> Hum Gurugram ke businesses ko **24 ghante mein verified staff** dete hain — per-hour shifts (1–12 hrs), payment kaam ke baad, instant replacement.
> Helper / waiter / kitchen / housekeeping / guard — jo chahiye bataiye: kitne log, kitne ghante?

*Keep it short and clean. Use the business owner's name if you have it.*

---

## 2. Call opening (for anyone who replies or opens)

> "Namaste, main [Name] Switch se. 30 second loonga —
> hum aapke jaise [restaurants] ko unke area ke verified staff dete hain, 24 ghante mein.
> **Abhi aapke yahan kitne log ki kami hai?**"

→ Let them talk. The moment they name a need, **lock the details:**
- Kitne log? (how many)
- Konsa role? (which role)
- **Kitne ghante? (1–12 hours)** — this is the unit we bill on
- Kab chahiye? (which day / time)
- Shift timing? (e.g. 10am–6pm)

> "Theek hai sir, main aaj hi [2] log [8] ghante ke shift ke liye bhej deta hoon.
> Aap bas interview le lijiyega. **Paisa tabhi jab koi join kare.** Deal?"

That "deal?" is your close. Get a yes.

---

## 3. Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Abhi zaroorat nahi" | "Koi baat nahi sir — agle hafte ya kabhi bhi turant chahiye ho to ek message. Main aapka number save kar leta hoon. Aam taur pe kitne logon ki team hai?" *(plant seed + learn churn)* |
| "Charges kya hai?" | "Sir bilkul transparent — placement fee tabhi jab banda join kare, advance kuch nahi. Number bhej dun details ke saath?" |
| "Staff tikta nahi" | "Sir aap to sirf shift ke liye le rahe ho (1–12 ghante) — lambe commitment ka tension hi nahi. Aur banda na aaye to instant replacement." |
| "Mahine ke liye chahiye" | "Sir hum monthly nahi karte — hum shift ke hisaab se dete hain, 1 se 12 ghante. Aapko jis din jitne ghante chahiye, bula lijiye. Zyada flexible aur sasta padta hai." |
| "Pehle se vendor hai" | "Bढ़िya — backup ke liye rakhiye. Jab unka banda nahi aata, hum 24 ghante mein cover karte hain. Ek baar try kar ke dekhiye." |
| "WhatsApp pe bhejo" | "Abhi bhejta hoon 👍" → send msg #1 immediately, then call back in 10 min. |

---

## 4. Follow-up (Day 2) — to every "maybe" from Day 1

> Sir namaste 🙏 [Name] from Switch.
> Aapke liye *[2 kitchen helpers]* ready hain — verified, *[area]* ke paas wale.
> Aaj interview ke liye bhej dun? Bas timing bata dijiye. ⚡

*Having a real candidate ready converts "maybe" → "yes" fastest.*

---

## 5. Referral ask (to every client who says yes)

> "Sir thank you 🙏 Aapke jaan-pehchaan mein koi aur restaurant/store owner hai jise staff chahiye?
> Ek number de dijiye, main unki bhi help kar doon."

---

## Daily rhythm

**Day 1**
- AM: WhatsApp the top 60 P1 leads (msg #1).
- All day: call everyone who opened/replied → lock requirements → mark Status.
- Walk into the 10 closest businesses in person.
- Target: **4–5 orders + ~15 warm**.

**Day 2**
- Call back every "maybe" with candidates ready (msg #4).
- Re-message non-responders once.
- Ask every closed client for a referral (#5).
- Target: **remaining 5–6 orders**.

> Update `2-day-blitz-callsheet.csv` after EVERY call. A lead you didn't log is a lead you'll lose.
